About Error 404

Error 404 is a game created by four frontend students at Medieinstitutet, united by a shard frustration - the internet’s most annoying phenomena. What happens if you gather everything that irritates you online and turn it into a game experience? That’s what we wanted to find out. In the game, you make your way through four rooms, each inspired ba a classic internet problem. The first room tests your patience with a complicated verification code. The second room throws you into a CAPTHA nightmare. The third room overwhelms you with complete popup chaos. And in the fourth and final room, a password so complicated it should be illegal awaits you. The game is built as a loving tribute to all the times the internet has made us want to throw our computer out the window - except this time, you’re supposed to have fun along the way.
